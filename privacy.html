Privacy Policy · Effective and last updated September 16, 2026

At a glance: Gameplay progress is stored locally. There is no app account, advertising, or third-party behavioral analytics. Apple processes purchases. Support email and website hosting are separate from gameplay and are explained below.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

STROBOFACTORY Inc. (株式会社ストロボファクトリー), Japan, develops and supports Ripplino: Calm Puzzles (formerly developed under the name CascadePuzzle). This policy explains how information is handled in the iPhone app, on this support website, and when you contact us. “We,” “us,” and “our” refer to STROBOFACTORY Inc.

For privacy questions, contact strobofactory@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

Ripplino stores stage progress, completed stages, game records such as chain counts and moves, tutorial status, and music, sound, and haptic preferences locally. The app also retains local information needed to display and verify access to purchased content.

These records support gameplay, saved progress, preferences, and purchase access. We do not receive your local gameplay records through a developer-operated server, and the app does not provide its own login account or cloud-sync service. Apple device backups may include app data according to your device and account settings.

3. Purchases and Apple services

Full Game Unlock is a non-consumable in-app purchase processed by Apple through StoreKit. The app uses verified product and transaction information, including purchase and revocation status, to grant or restore access to stages 16–60. This information is not sent by Ripplino to our own purchase server.

We do not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account password from the app. Apple separately processes purchases, restoration, fraud prevention, refunds, and account information under its own policies. Apple may provide us with sales reports, aggregate app information, or diagnostics according to its services and your settings. See App Store & Privacy and Apple’s privacy policy.

4. Advertising, analytics, and permissions

Ripplino contains no advertising SDK, third-party behavioral analytics SDK, or cross-app advertising tracking. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. The app does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or health data for gameplay.

The music and sound effects are bundled with the app. Playing them does not send your gameplay or audio to Suno, ElevenLabs, or another generative-AI service. This describes the shipped app, not the services used to produce its media.

5. Information you choose to send to support

If you email support, we receive your email address, the message, and any screenshots, recordings, or device details you choose to include. We use these to answer your request, investigate reported problems, and maintain support records. We do not add you to a marketing list because you asked for help.

Email is handled by our email provider, Google (Gmail), as well as your own email provider. We limit disclosure to providers needed to handle the request, disclosures you request, and disclosures necessary to comply with applicable law or protect legal rights. Please do not send passwords, verification codes, payment-card details, or unnecessary sensitive information. See Google’s privacy policy.

6. This website and external links

This is a static support website prepared for GitHub Pages hosting. It uses no advertising scripts, analytics tags, embedded third-party media, external web fonts, or site-managed cookies. GitHub, as the hosting provider, logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, including when visitors are not signed in. Its handling of hosting information is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement and GitHub Pages data-collection notice.

Following an external link or sending an email opens another service with its own privacy practices. We do not control those services’ handling of information.

7. Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local game information remains on your device until it is removed through available app controls, deletion of the app, or device-data management. Offloading an app is not the same as deleting its data. Copies in Apple-managed backups are controlled by your backup settings. We cannot retrieve or remotely delete local-only game records from our servers because we do not hold those records there.

Deleting or resetting the app does not erase Apple’s purchase history. Purchase access is handled separately through Apple and StoreKit.

We keep support correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to resolve the request, follow up, maintain relevant support records, or meet applicable legal obligations. You may ask us to access, correct, or delete information you sent us by email. Depending on where you live, additional privacy rights may apply. We will assess requests under applicable law and may retain information where a legal obligation or legitimate unresolved matter requires it. We cannot delete records held independently by Apple, GitHub, or your email provider.

8. Security and international processing

We take reasonable steps to limit access to support correspondence and use it only for the purposes described here. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security. Do not include unnecessary confidential information in support requests.

We operate in Japan. Apple, GitHub, Google, and their service providers may process information in other countries under their respective policies. Using this app does not give us access to your Apple Account credentials.

9. Children’s privacy

Ripplino does not ask children to create an account or provide personal information to play. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support address, please contact us so we can review and, where appropriate, delete it. Parents or guardians can manage purchase permissions through Apple’s settings.

10. Updates and contact

We may update this policy when the app’s practices or applicable requirements change. The current version and update date will be shown on this page. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent before a material change.
