Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Tap a core to add energy. When it fills, it releases a ripple that charges neighboring cores. Prepare a few cores, then connect their ripples into a chain. Each stage shows its own goal: reach a chain length or collect marked targets.

Stage 1 is a no-fail introduction. Later stages have a move limit, but no countdown clock. Moves shows the moves remaining.

What do Bloom, Echo, and Prism do?

Normal cores send ripples up, down, left, and right. Bloom also reaches the four diagonal neighbors. Echo sends a second ripple in the four straight directions. Prism sends ripples only along the four diagonals.

Bloom’s extra diagonal ripple and Echo’s second ripple activate once per core, per tap-and-chain sequence. If that same special core fills again during the sequence, it sends only the normal four-direction ripple. The special ability becomes available again on your next tap.

What does Full Game Unlock include?

Stages 1–15 are free. One non-consumable in-app purchase unlocks access to stages 16–60. Normal stage progression still applies: purchasing removes the paid-content restriction, not the requirement to complete earlier stages.

There are no ads, subscriptions, or consumable purchases. Check the price displayed by the App Store purchase sheet for your region before confirming.

I already purchased. How do I restore access?

Connect to the internet and use the same Apple Account that made the purchase. Open the app’s Full Game purchase screen and select Restore Purchases. Allow Apple to complete its verification.

Restoring a purchase restores access to paid content; it does not recover local stage progress. If access is still missing, contact us before deleting the app. See Apple’s purchase restoration guide.

My purchase is pending, canceled, or unsuccessful.

A pending request, including Ask to Buy, does not unlock the full game until Apple approves and verifies the purchase. A canceled or failed attempt does not grant access. After the current operation has finished, you can try again.

If a charge appears but access is missing, try Restore Purchases and contact us. Never send your Apple Account password, verification code, or card details.

Why can’t I hear the music or sound effects?

Ripplino respects your iPhone’s Silent Mode. Turn Silent Mode off, check the device volume and audio output route, and check Music and Sound Effects in the app’s Settings. These two settings work independently; Haptics is separate.

If sound does not return after unlocking your phone, try switching Music off and on. If needed, close and reopen the app. Please report the device model, iOS version, and steps that led to the issue. Do not delete the app just to troubleshoot audio.

Where is my progress saved?

Stage progress, records, and preferences are stored on your device. Ripplino does not provide its own account or cloud-sync service. Apple device backups may include app data depending on your settings.

Deleting the app or resetting progress can remove local records. Purchase restoration and game-progress restoration are different. Contact us before reinstalling if you need help preserving progress.

Can I play offline?

Puzzle gameplay works offline. Downloading the app, buying Full Game Unlock, and restoring a purchase may require an internet connection and Apple’s services.

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles App Store payment and refund requests. Visit Apple’s Report a Problem page and follow the instructions for your purchase. Refund eligibility is determined by Apple and applicable rules; we cannot promise approval.

How do I report a bug?

Email us with the app version, iPhone model, iOS version, stage number, and what happened just before the issue. A screenshot or short recording is helpful but optional. Remove personal information before attaching it.

If the board appears stuck, capture the screen before using Restart. Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment details, or unrelated personal data.
